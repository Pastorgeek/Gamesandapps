How do I get these? (one-time setup, about 30 minutes)
  1. In the Instagram app, switch the account to a Professional account (Business or Creator): Settings → Account type and tools.
  2. Link it to a Facebook Page (for example, your church or youth group page): Page settings → Linked accounts → Instagram.
  3. Go to developers.facebook.com/apps, choose Create app, and pick the Business type.
  4. Open the Graph API Explorer, select your app, and add these permissions: instagram_basic, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, business_management. Click Generate Access Token and log in.
  5. To make the token last about 60 days, open the Access Token Debugger, paste the token, and click Extend Access Token.
  6. Paste the token above and click Save token & find my account, then pick your Instagram account.
  7. Hashtag search uses Meta's Instagram Public Content Access feature. If you get a permission error, request it in your app's dashboard under App Review → Permissions and Features.

Limit: 30 different hashtags per 7 days. Searching the same hashtag again doesn't count.
